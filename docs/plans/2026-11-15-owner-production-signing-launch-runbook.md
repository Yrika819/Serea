## Recovery and incident response

Missing/corrupt/mismatched Vault, Ledger, Registry, publication receipt, backup, candidate, provenance, or signer output is **UNKNOWN/STOP**, never evidence of a fresh identity. `ledger-recovery-plan` is non-destructive; `ledger-abandon` refuses an attempt while its managed receipt exists. `ledger-recover-publish` is an explicit-confirmation, Disposable-only operation for an exact latest `ATTEMPTED`/`BUILT` attempt with a `VERIFIED`/`BUILT` receipt and verified stored APK. It can reconcile Vault artifact history, append a missing `BUILT`, and finish no-replace sidecar-then-APK publication; it never re-signs. Unknown or conflicting state is a hard stop. Do not initialize replacement state, edit JSON/Ledger, decrement a version, delete evidence, or mark unverified output successful. A failed or interrupted signature consumes its version code. This recovery operation has not yet been proven on an encrypted DMG with actual mount/unmount or under true competing processes.

On suspected key/password exposure, stop signing and isolate the host/backups under the Owner’s incident process. Do not use Keychain secrets or place passwords in argv, environment variables, Gradle properties, shell history, files, chat, or logs. The future signer must request the passphrase interactively without echo and never forward it to Gradle.
